Free games, right in your browser

Crumbly Games is a small collection of games for a quick break: no download, no install and no paywall.

What you'll find here

All games run directly on the page, on your computer, tablet or phone. Just open one and start playing. They are easy to learn, but not so easy to master.

Crumb Clicker

Click crumbs, buy upgrades and grow your crumb empire. Rebirths let you start over even stronger, and the leaderboard shows how you stack up against other players.

Platformer

Jump, dodge obstacles and collect coins. There are many levels that get harder as you go, plus a menu and a level select so you can pick up where you left off.

Saving and leaderboards

You can try the games without signing in. If you sign in with your Google account, your progress is saved in the cloud so you can keep playing on another device.

  • The leaderboard only shows your Google name. Your email address stays private.
  • Signing in is optional. Every game can be played without an account.
  • You can read how data is handled in the privacy policy (in German).

Why it's free

Crumbly Games costs nothing to play. To cover the domain and hosting, the site shows ads through Google AdSense. That keeps every game free for everyone, and we try to keep the ads from getting in the way of playing.

Who makes this?

The site is built and maintained by a single developer. New levels, design improvements and new features are added all the time. If you spot a bug or have an idea for a new level or game, get in touch. Contact details are in the legal notice (in German).

Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. The games run in any modern browser, such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox or Edge.

Does it cost anything?

No. There are no purchases and no subscription.

My progress is gone. What can I do?

Without signing in, progress is only stored in your browser, so clearing your browser data deletes it. Sign in with Google to keep your progress safe.

I found a bug.

Send a short message using the contact details in the legal notice and tell us what happened and on which device.
